Direction for use

Four steps to a sealed surface

Straight from the pack. Follow the recommended application method and the forced coverage rates for the full 15-year performance.

Prepare the surface

Clean and dry the surface thoroughly. Repair defects with Simba Crack Filler or Repair Master before you start.

Self-priming coat

Apply a self-priming coat of Simba Damp Shield diluted to 40%. Let it key into the substrate.

Undiluted coats

Two undiluted coats on the terrace, one undiluted coat on walls — applied at forced coverage.

Finish

Texture is optional on exterior and interior walls, followed by 2–3 coats of Simba Wall Top Coat.

Questions

Before you buy

How long does Simba Damp Shield last?
The coating is formulated to deliver 15 years of waterproofing when it is applied over a properly prepared surface at the recommended forced coverage — a priming coat at 40% dilution followed by two undiluted coats on terraces.
Can it really reduce my terrace temperature by 12°?
The cured film is highly solar-reflective, so the slab absorbs far less heat through the day. The result is a noticeably cooler top floor and a lower cooling load. The exact drop depends on your roof colour, slab thickness, insulation and local conditions.
My terrace already has cracks. Do I need to repair them first?
Yes. Clean and dry the surface and fill defects with Simba Crack Filler or Repair Master before coating. Once the surface is sound, the elastomeric film bridges movement cracks up to 2 mm and keeps them sealed as the slab expands and contracts.
Can I apply it myself, or do I need a contractor?
A careful DIY application works on small areas if you follow the four steps and the coverage rates exactly. For full terraces we recommend a trained applicator — forced coverage and film thickness are what deliver the performance. Call us and we will guide you or point you to a dealer.
Does it work on interior walls too?
Yes. Simba Damp Shield is made for both exterior and interior wall surfaces as well as terraces. On walls, apply the priming coat followed by one undiluted coat, then optional texture and 2–3 coats of Simba Wall Top Coat.
Is it safe to use at home?
Lead content does not exceed 90 parts per million. As with any coating, apply in a well-ventilated area and keep the pack away from children. Full precautions are printed on the pack — scan the QR code on the front for the complete product information.
